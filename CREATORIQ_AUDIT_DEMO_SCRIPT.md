# CreatorIQ audit demo — July 28

Open:

`http://localhost:3412/dashboard?view=audits&auditType=website&demo=creator-iq`

## 90-second walkthrough

**1. Set the frame — 15 seconds**

“I use this workflow to turn a broad website review into an evidence-backed decision tool. This is a replay of the actual July 25 CreatorIQ run: 15 homepage-visible buyer-journey pages, each rendered on desktop and mobile, plus Lighthouse for both. The result stays provisional because evidence coverage is 69%.”

**2. Show evidence and scoring — 30 seconds**

Open the CreatorIQ walkthrough. Point to evidence coverage, then the category scores.

“The checklist score is 70, with a target of 75. It only uses verified passes and failures; unverified items do not quietly become passes. Lighthouse stays separate at 39 mobile and 65 desktop so technical performance informs priorities without changing the UX score formula.”

Open **Content** or **Design & Typography**.

“The report found 26 verified failures across the product, proof, resource, and conversion journey. The clearest risks are form labeling and grouping, undersized mobile controls, contrast and accessible names, heading and image semantics, and slow mobile performance. I can open any category to show the observed metric, affected URL, and exact fix.”

**3. Move from finding to action — 30 seconds**

Point to the specific evidence, affected URL, and fix, then choose **View prioritised plan**.

“Each failed criterion carries its source and a specific next move. I would start with shared demo-form labels and grouping, standardize mobile control sizing, repair contrast and accessible names, then address the homepage’s 24.9-second mobile LCP and layout shift.”

**4. Close — 15 seconds**

“The value is the handoff: Demand Generation gets a prioritized backlog tied to conversion and lead quality, design gets clear UX direction, and development gets bounded work that can be measured and rerun.”

## If asked what you would do next

- Complete keyboard and form-completion testing, then verify analytics and CRM event coverage.
- Run the dedicated SEO pass for search intent, keyword coverage, internal linking, structured data, and indexation; those remain outside this public-page website result.
- Confirm the primary conversion KPI and HubSpot lifecycle events with Demand Generation.
- Turn the top verified findings into a two-week test backlog.
- Rerun the audit after launch and compare evidence coverage, checklist score, and qualified-demo conversion.
